I am an AI researcher and engineer working on post-training, evaluation, and agent RL. My research approach is to understand complex AI systems through their failure modes and build feedback loops that improve them. In practice, this means 1) real, high-quality environments such as Terminal-Bench; 2) granular and reliable feedbacks such as rubrics; and 3) flexible infrastructure that lowers noise while raising fault tolerance.

Beyond model and eval development, I’m also interested in AI’s broader impact on humanity. When I am tired with blackboxes, I also follow interpretability work that tries to understand these systems mechanistically or statistically.
