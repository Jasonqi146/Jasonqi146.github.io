Research

My recent research explores how people reason, make decisions, and form expectations about the future. In particular, I study how mid-training and reinforcement learning can help language models simulate these processes. I focus on scaling these simulations across diverse individuals and situations, and on building realistic human simulators for training and evaluating long-horizon agents.

Ultimately, I hope to build models that reflect the richness and diversity of human behavior, with the ambition of making realistic simulation possible for any person, in any context.
