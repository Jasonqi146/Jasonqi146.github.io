Reward models are not just classifiers over preferences. In production post-training loops, they become part of the feedback infrastructure: they decide what gets optimized, how failures are surfaced, and how quickly model teams can iterate.

At Scale AI, I worked on SFT and GRPO post-training for LLM judge and reward models, with large evaluation and training sets spanning instruction following, coding, STEM, multilingual, and multimodal reasoning capabilities.

The useful part of this work was the full loop: designing evaluation sets, diagnosing judge behavior, improving the model, and connecting the result back to human-AI data pipelines. A reward model is only as valuable as the operational system around it.
