In industry evaluation work, the hard part is often not writing one clever rubric. It is building the whole oversight loop: task design, annotation workflows, rubric diagnostics, automated rating, human review, disagreement analysis, and production monitoring.

At Scale AI, I worked on scalable oversight systems for reward and preference pipelines across multilingual, instruction-following, multimodal reasoning, STEM, and coding tasks. This included automated rating services, A/B-tested metrics, and quality-control agents using retrieval, tool use, and multi-agent debate.

The goal was to turn human judgment into a more reliable system rather than a bottleneck. Good oversight should lower noise, preserve useful disagreement, and make failure modes visible before they become training signal.
